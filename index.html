Our Story

Little moments deserve a little more magic.

It started with one very balloon-obsessed toddler.

Balloon Studio actually began with a toddler who was very serious about balloons. Every day, we'd get him one. He'd spend the whole day carrying it around, playing with it and being completely obsessed with it. Then, by evening, he'd happily let it go and watch it disappear into the sky. Balloon gone. Toddler ready for another one tomorrow.

After enough balloons, we thought... maybe there's something here.

What started as a little everyday ritual turned into the idea behind Balloon Studio.
